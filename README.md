# The Veil
A under-populated patch of sky found on `23-5-2026`, cross checked by catalogs of:
- SDSS Spectroscopy
- SDSS Photometry
- SIMBAD

## What you can do
As a nerd or astronomer, you can try to reproduce the findings or cross check even more
catalogs.  
Useful data:
- Ra span: ~ 249.92 - 252.08 degrees (Delta RA = 2.16 degrees)
- Dec span: ~ 54.6 - 65 degrees (Delta DEC = 10.4 degrees)
- z(redshift) span: ~ 0.00009 - 6z (NOTE: 6z was the survey limit, so it could extend further)

**NOTE:** Dec showed it spanned for `54.6deg to 90` but as survey coverage drops after ~65
I am taking 65 as the limit here.
